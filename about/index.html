Our Story

Born From a
Passion for Perfection

AquaLux was founded in 2018 by Alexander Reed, a former automotive engineer who was frustrated by the lack of truly premium car wash options in Los Angeles.

He envisioned a car wash that combined luxury spa-level service with cutting-edge automotive technology — a place where every vehicle, from a Honda to a Ferrari, receives the same obsessive attention to detail.

Our Journey

2018

Founded in Beverly Hills

AquaLux opened its first location with 3 employees and a vision to redefine car wash standards in Los Angeles.

2019

Ceramic Coating Launch

We became one of the first car washes in LA to offer professional-grade ceramic coating services.

2020

Digital Transformation

Launched our online booking platform and contactless service options during challenging times.

2021

West Hollywood Expansion

Opened our second location in West Hollywood, doubling our capacity and team size.

2022

Membership Program

Launched the AquaLux Loyalty Club, reaching 1,000 members within the first 6 months.

2023

Fleet Services Division

Established our dedicated fleet services division, partnering with 12 major businesses.

2024

Santa Monica Location

Third location opens near the beach — our most popular spot yet with ocean air car care.

2026

Franchise Launch

AquaLux opens its franchise program — inviting entrepreneurs to join our growing family.
