Pricing FAQ

Is pricing different for larger vehicles?

Yes! SUVs cost ~20% more, Trucks ~30% more, and Luxury/Exotic vehicles ~50% more than the base Sedan price. Our booking system calculates this automatically.

Do prices include tax?

All listed prices are before applicable sales tax. Tax is calculated at checkout based on your location.

Can I add services after booking?

Yes! Contact us via WhatsApp or call us before your appointment to add services.

Is there a membership discount?

Members save 15–30% on all services. See our Membership page for details.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash. Secure checkout via Stripe.
